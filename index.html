Our story

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit, founded in 2020 and based in Chhattisgarh. What began as a small effort to support local children's education has grown into six community-facing initiatives spanning education, women's empowerment, environmental action, animal welfare, employability training, and food & clothing relief.

Our approach stays close to the ground: we work directly with the communities we serve, rely on volunteer networks across the country, and treat every rupee donated as a thread we're accountable for.

The website you're looking at right now is itself a work in progress — this redesign was built as part of an internal UX audit to make it easier for donors and volunteers to find, trust, and support the cause that speaks to them.
